Arc 1: The Seed of Silence
Chapter 14: The Rust Throne

The Rust Tower didn't have a door. It had a mouth.

The entrance was a jagged tear in the iron wall, shaped like a scream. The air coming out of it was hot and smelled of old grease.

"Are you sure about this?" Sylas whispered. He had left his glowing suit behind at the Walker camp. Now he was wearing a cloak made of dark **Glow-Moss** that blended in with the shadows.

"No," Leo admitted. "But the Mist-Walkers said the thimble is inside. And if we don't get it, we don't fix the engine. And if we don't fix the engine, we don't get home."

They stepped inside.

The interior of the tower was a vertical maze of pipes, gears, and catwalks. It was dark, lit only by the faint red pulse of the **Rust Signal**.

*Thrum. Thrum. Thrum.*

The sound was louder here. It vibrated in Leo's teeth. He touched his silver thimble. It was cold, acting as a shield against the heat of the entropy.

"Look," Sylas breathed, pointing up.

Hanging from the ceiling like bats were massive, iron shapes. They had claws made of hydraulic pincers and eyes that flickered with a dim, orange light.

**Steam-Beasts.**

They were asleep. But their sleep wasn't peaceful. They twitched and shuddered, their gears grinding together in a metallic snore.

"They're having nightmares," Leo realized. "They remember the war."

"If we wake them up," Sylas said, "they will crush us."

"Then we don't wake them up," Leo said. "We soothe them."

He walked to the nearest support pillar. It was covered in Grey Lines. The tower itself was stressed. It was holding up millions of tons of iron, and it was tired.

Leo placed his hand on the metal.

*Smooth. Quiet. Rest.*

He sent a Pulse of Peace into the structure. It wasn't a strong pulse. It was a lullaby. He visualized oiling the gears. He imagined loosening the bolts just enough to relieve the pressure.

Above them, a Steam-Beast let out a long, hissing sigh. Its orange eyes went dark. Its claws relaxed.

"It worked," Sylas whispered. "You put the building to sleep."

"I just made it comfortable," Leo said, wiping sweat from his forehead. "Let's move."

They climbed. Up and up, past floors of silent machinery and dormant furnaces. The air got hotter. The red light got brighter.

Finally, they reached the top. The **Rust Throne**.

It wasn't a chair. It was a control room. In the center of the room was a massive, brass heart. It was beating. Every beat sent a shockwave of red light through the tower.

And stuck in the center of the heart, jamming the main gear, was a tiny, shining object.

**The Second Thimble.**

"There it is," Leo said.

He walked toward the heart. The heat was intense. It felt like walking into an oven. The Grey Lines here were thick as ropes.

"Leo, wait," Sylas warned. "If you pull that out, the gear will turn."

"I know," Leo said. "That's the point. The thimble stopped the machine five hundred years ago. But the machine is trying to start again. It's fighting the jam. That's what the signal is. It's the sound of the gear trying to crush the silver."

Leo reached out. His hand was shaking. He could feel the "Scream" of the machine. It wanted to move. It wanted to be loud.

He grabbed the thimble.

It burned his fingers. It was hot with five centuries of friction.

"Sorry," Leo whispered to the machine. "But you've been stuck long enough."

He pulled.

*PING.*

The thimble came free.

The brass heart stopped beating. The red light vanished. For a second, there was absolute silence.

Then, the gear turned.

*CLICK.*

It didn't scream. It clicked into place. The machine didn't explode. It simply... shifted. The massive brass heart opened like a flower.

And inside, sitting on a cushion of velvet dust, was not a weapon. It was a seed. A **Golden Seed** from the Sun-Tree.

"It wasn't a factory," Leo whispered, staring at the seed. "It was an incubator."

The Iron Kings hadn't been building a weapon. They had been trying to grow a new Sun.
