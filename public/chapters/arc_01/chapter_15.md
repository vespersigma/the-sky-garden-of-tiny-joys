Arc 1: The Seed of Silence
Chapter 15: The Ascent

The climb down from the Rust Throne was silent.

Leo carried the Golden Seed wrapped in his Current-Cloak. It was warm, pulsing against his chest like a second heart. Sylas walked ahead, using his glowing suit to light the catwalks.

They reached the Mist Lake without waking the Steam-Beasts. The fog was waiting for them, thick and silver.

"We have a problem," Sylas whispered, looking at the *Drifting Leaf*.

The sky-boat was still floating where they had crashed, but the **Quiet Engine** was in pieces. The acorn was cracked down the middle, leaking faint traces of mana into the air. The grey wire was dull and lifeless.

"We can't fly," Sylas said. "The currents down here are too weak for sails. Without the engine, we're stuck."

Leo knelt by the broken device. He felt the weight of the Seed in his arms. He felt the weight of the two Silver Thimbles around his neck.

"We can fix it," Leo said.

"It's shattered, Leo," Sylas argued. "The capacitor blew. The acorn couldn't handle the feedback loop."

"That's because we didn't have a regulator," Leo said. He pulled the **Second Thimble** from his cord.

It was made of Moon-Silver, cold and heavy. It had stopped a machine the size of a city for five hundred years. It knew how to handle pressure.

Leo placed the cracked acorn on the deck. He touched the split shell.

*Mend.*

He didn't just push the pieces together. He visualized the "Idea" of the nut. He reminded it of the Muffin-Mice and their gratitude. The shell knit itself back together with a soft *click*.

Then, he took the Second Thimble. He placed it over the tip of the acorn, capping it like a tiny helmet.

"The wire goes here," Leo muttered, wrapping the grey coil around the silver.

As the wire touched the thimble, the engine didn't just hum. It **Sang**.

It was a pure, clear note that cut through the fog. The mist around the boat began to swirl, pushed back by the sheer pressure of the harmony.

"Is that safe?" Sylas asked, stepping back.

"It's grounded," Leo said. "The thimble acts as a filter. It catches the excess noise and turns it into thrust."

He looked at Sylas. "Get us out of here."

Sylas grinned. He jumped to the helm and grabbed the Wind-Lute. He played a C-Major chord.

The engine flared with white light.

*WHOOSH.*

This time, the acceleration wasn't violent. It was smooth. The boat lifted out of the lake, shedding droplets of liquid memory. It rose into the grey sky, climbing faster and faster.

They punched through the cloud layer.

One second, they were in the dark. The next, they were blinded by gold.

They burst into the Middle Reach. The sun was shining. The sky was an impossible blue. The air smelled of jasmine and pine.

Below them, the Petal-Wharf islands were drifting peacefully. The **Rust Signal** was gone. The world was quiet.

"We made it," Sylas laughed, throwing his hands in the air.

Leo didn't cheer. He unwrapped his cloak and looked at the Golden Seed. In the sunlight, it was glowing so brightly it was hard to look at.

"We didn't just make it," Leo said. "We brought the sunrise with us."
