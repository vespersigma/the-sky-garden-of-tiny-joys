Arc 4: The Festival of Colors
Chapter 3: The Sabotage

The day of the Bake-Off arrived with a fanfare of trumpets.

Master Crumb had set up his station in the center of the village square. It looked less like a bakery and more like a fortress. The **Gravity-Whisk 3000** was humming aggressively, its brass gears spinning so fast they were a blur.

"Observe!" Crumb shouted to the crowd. "The perfect emulsion of fat and sugar!"

On the other side of the stage, Mina was quietly grating the **Moon-Vanilla** into her bowl. The scent was incredible. It drifted through the air like a visible ribbon of white mist, calming everyone who smelled it.

"He's nervous," Leo whispered to Barnaby. They were standing in the front row, cheering for Mina.

"How can you tell?" Barnaby hummed. "He is very... shiny."

"His machine is vibrating," Leo said. "Listen."

Underneath the roar of the whisk, there was a high-pitched whine. *Eeeeeeeee.* It sounded like a mosquito trapped in a jar.

"The bearings are misaligned," Leo diagnosed. "He didn't account for the humidity of the Stamen Well."

Suddenly, the whine turned into a scream.

*SCREEEEEECH.*

Smoke poured out of Crumb's machine. The Gravity-Whisk shuddered violently, rattling across the table. A bolt shot out of the casing and pinged off Barnaby's chest.

"Sabotage!" Crumb screamed, pointing a flour-covered finger at Leo. "The Mender broke it! I saw him looking at it with his... his eyes!"

The crowd gasped. The machine was now shaking so hard it was cracking the wooden stage.

"It's going to explode," Sylas yelled. "Everyone back!"

"No!" Crumb wailed. "My emulsion! My legacy!"

Leo didn't run away. He ran toward the stage.

"Leo, wait!" Mina called out.

Leo vaulted onto the platform. The heat coming off the machine was intense. It smelled of burning oil and panicked ambition.

"Get away from it!" Crumb shouted. "You've done enough!"

"I haven't done anything yet," Leo said calmly.

He reached out. He didn't use the thimble. He placed his bare hand on the vibrating brass casing.

*Stop.*

He sent a Pulse of Peace into the machine. It wasn't a command; it was a question.

*Where does it hurt?*

The machine answered. It wasn't a voice. It was a sensation of twisting, grinding pain in the central drive shaft. The metal was screaming because it was being forced to spin faster than its nature allowed.

"It's not broken," Leo announced to the crowd. "It's dizzy."

He visualized the drive shaft. He imagined it slowing down, taking a deep breath, and finding its center. He smoothed the friction between the gears. He cooled the overheated oil with a thought of **Black-Ice Tea**.

The screeching stopped. The shaking ceased.

The machine let out a long, contented sigh. The gears slowed to a gentle, rhythmic tick-tock.

*Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock.*

It sounded like a heartbeat.

Leo pulled his hand back. "There. I adjusted the torque. It should be fine now."

He looked at Crumb. The master baker was staring at him, his mouth open.

"Why?" Crumb whispered. "You could have let it break. You could have won."

Leo wiped a smudge of oil from his hand.

"I'm a Mender," Leo said. "I fix things. Even the things I don't like."

He walked back to Mina's station. She was smiling at him.

"Show-off," she whispered.

"I just hate loud noises," Leo said, picking up a wooden spoon. "Now, let's bake a cake."
