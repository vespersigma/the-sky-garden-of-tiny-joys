Arc 2: The Current War
Chapter 5: The New Compact

The return to Petal-Wharf was slow.

Sylas piloted the boat with one hand, his other arm wrapped in bandages made of **Petal-Silk**. Barnaby sat at the prow, his chest still glowing faintly from the blast he had absorbed. And Leo lay on the deck, staring up at the newly flowing Blue Current.

"They're following us," Sylas said, glancing back.

Three black ships were trailing them. They weren't attacking. They were escorting.

"Let them come," Leo said. "We owe them a cup of tea."

When they landed, the village was silent. The ice wall had shattered, raining shards of black frost onto the roofs of the Growing Houses. Elder Thorne stood at the dock, his staff raised. Behind him, the entire village watched with bated breath.

Leo stepped off the boat. He stumbled, but Barnaby caught him.

"Easy, Mender," the golem hummed. "You are still heavy."

The Silence-Keepers landed. Their leader walked down the ramp. His bone mask was cracked down the middle, a result of the whale's song.

He stopped in front of Leo.

"You broke the ice," the Keeper said. His voice was no longer flat. It sounded tired.

"And you tried to freeze the sun," Leo replied. "We both made mistakes."

The Keeper looked at the sky, where the Blue Current was rushing freely toward the horizon.

"The Whales are singing again," the Keeper admitted. "We heard them. For the first time in a century, the deep sky is not silent."

"The engine was a mistake," Leo said. "I built it to be efficient. I forgot to make it sustainable. It drank too much."

He reached into his pocket and pulled out the **Silver Thimble**.

"But I know how to fix it," Leo continued. "We don't need to stop the currents. We need to regulate them. I can build a filter. A way to slow the flow just enough to feed the Whales without starving the villages."

The Keeper hesitated. He looked at Thorne, then at the villagers, then at the cracked mask in his reflection in the water.

"We are the Keepers of Silence," he said. "We do not trust machines."

"Trust the Mender," Elder Thorne said, stepping forward. "He did not fight you with a weapon. He fought you with a song."

Thorne gestured to the table that was still sitting on the dock. The teapot was cold. The white cloth was stained brown.

"Sit," Leo said. "Please."

The Keeper looked at the table. Slowly, he sat down.

Leo didn't use the Truth-Root this time. He reached into his own bag. He pulled out a small jar of **Black-Ice Leaves**, a rare tea he had found in the Mist Lakes.

He brewed it with cold water.

He poured a cup for the Keeper. The liquid was dark and smelled of snow.

"It's bitter," Leo warned. "But it wakes you up."

The Keeper lifted his mask just enough to reveal a pale, scarred chin. He took a sip.

He didn't pour it out.

"It is... clean," the Keeper said.

He reached into his robe and pulled out a small object. It was a shard of **Star-Metal**. He placed it on the table.

"If you are to mend the currents," the Keeper said, "you will need metal that does not rust."

Leo picked up the shard. It was heavy, but it hummed with a frequency that matched the Blue Current perfectly.

"Deal," Leo said.

The Keeper stood up. "We will return to the Shadow Border. We will watch the Whales. If the song stops again... we will return."

"If the song stops," Leo promised, "I'll be the first one to fix it."

The black ships rose into the air. They turned and vanished into the northern sky, leaving behind a chill that was quickly warmed by the golden light of the Sun-Tree.

Mina ran onto the dock. She didn't say anything. She just hugged Leo so hard his ribs creaked.

"You're an idiot," she whispered into his shoulder.

"I know," Leo said, resting his chin on her flour-dusted hair. "But I'm a mender. We learn by breaking things."

**End of Arc 2**
